# Risk Management & Security

CloudBank employs a "Multi-Layer Isolation + Real-Time Monitoring + Automated Defense" architecture.

### Multi-Layer Security Defense

* Multi-Party Audits: All core modules (Matching Engine, Liquidation, Oracle, etc.) must undergo minimum two independent audits.
* Automated Defense: Real-time simulations and dynamic security networks.

### Market and Liquidity Risk

* Dynamic Liquidation: Features partial deleveraging and automated delta-hedging to prevent total position loss.
* Dynamic Funding Rate: Automatically adjusted based on Open Interest (OI) and volatility to maintain delta neutrality.
* Strict Isolation: CLP and GLP funds are strictly isolated; community pool risks do not affect the official pool.

### User-Layer Risk

* Market Risk: Traders bear risks associated with price fluctuations.
* Smart Contract Risk: While audited, residual risks of vulnerabilities exist. Core contracts are non-upgradable.
* Network Stability Risk: Risks related to blockchain congestion or disruption.
