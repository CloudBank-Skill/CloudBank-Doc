# Global Liquidity Pool (GLP)

The **GLP** is the foundational liquidity layer maintained by the protocol.\
It provides consistent depth, stability, and risk absorption across the entire CloudBank ecosystem.

#### **Key Characteristics**

* Provides deep liquidity for major assets (e.g., BTC, ETH, USDT)
* Supports perpetual trading and high-volume transactions
* Acts as the stability layer for the entire system
* Absorbs volatility and safeguards against liquidity fragmentation

#### **GLP Revenue Sources**

* Trading fees
* Funding rates from perpetual contracts
* Liquidation fees
* Shared revenue from other connected modules

The GLP ensures CloudBank’s core markets remain efficient and resilient, even during extreme market conditions.
