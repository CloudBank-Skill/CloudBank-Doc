# Community Liquidity Pool (CLP)

The **CLP** empowers _any community, project, creator, or KOL_ to independently launch and operate their own liquidity pools and markets.

#### **Key Characteristics**

* Permissionless creation of trading pairs
* Customizable liquidity rules and market parameters
* Revenue flows to community LPs and pool creators
* Ideal for long-tail assets and emerging communities
* Can integrate incentives, badges, quests, and token rewards

#### **Use Cases for CLP**

* KOLs launching their own token markets
* Projects bootstrapping liquidity for new assets
* Niche communities building specialized markets
* Tokenized micro-economies for Web3 groups

The CLP allows communities to build decentralized economies without depending on centralized exchanges or liquidity providers.
