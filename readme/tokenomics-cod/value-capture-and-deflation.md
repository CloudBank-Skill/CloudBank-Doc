# Value Capture & Deflation

Protocol Revenue Distribution:

R(burn) = 20% \* R

R(stakers) = 40% \* R

R(treasury) = 10% \* R

R(team) = 10% \* R

R(insurance) = 20% \* R

Buyback & Burn: The protocol mandates a buyback and burn of at least 20% of revenue to enforce continuous deflation and scarcity.
