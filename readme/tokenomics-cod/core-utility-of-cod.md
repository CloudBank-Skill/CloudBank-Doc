# Core Utility of COD

COD fulfills six core functions:

1. Trading Fee Discount: Staking COD reduces fees based on the formula $$Discount(S) = min(40\%, k \cdot ln(1+S))$$.
2. Governance: Vote on liquidity parameters, risk models, and treasury allocation. Voting weight is boosted by stake duration (VoteWeight = StakedCOD \cdot TimeBoost).
3. Launchpad Access: A tiered system (Lv.1 to Lv.4) requiring COD staking to access new project quotas.
4. Staking Rewards (Real Yield): Stakers receive a share of "Protocol Cash Flow" (fees, funding rates, AI yields), independent of inflation.
5. Ecosystem Collateral: COD serves as margin for creating CLPs and collateral for contract markets.
