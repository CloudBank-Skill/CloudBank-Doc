# Decentralized Exchange (DEX)

The CloudBank DEX supports both spot and perpetual trading, powered by liquidity aggregated from GLP, CLP, and cross-chain sources.

#### **Key Features**

* Spot trading for major and long-tail assets
* Perpetual trading with institutional-grade pricing
* Aggregated liquidity from multiple chains and pools
* Low slippage and high capital efficiency

#### **Technology Highlights**

* Index-based oracle pricing to prevent manipulation
* Unified order engine with optimized execution
* Maker/taker liquidity incentives
* Multi-pool routing for best price discovery

The CloudBank DEX forms the backbone of the ecosystem, ensuring seamless trading across all user-created and system markets.
