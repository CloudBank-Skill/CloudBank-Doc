# Prediction Markets

CloudBank includes a high-performance prediction market engine supporting event-driven market creation.

#### **Key Features**

* Markets covering price movements, sports, social events, governance outcomes and more
* Permissionless creation of prediction events
* Liquidity sourced from CLP and GLP
* Real-time, manipulation-resistant pricing via index oracles

#### **Cold-Start Advantages**

* GLP provides baseline liquidity for early markets
* Communities can create their own event markets via CLP
* AI engines provide probability insights to enhance engagement

Prediction markets extend CloudBank’s trading layer into event-based economic systems.
