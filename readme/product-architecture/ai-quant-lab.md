# AI Quant Lab

The AI Quant Lab is CloudBank’s automated strategy framework, designed to bring professional-grade algorithmic trading to everyday users.

#### **Core Capabilities**

* AI-powered strategy generation
* Multi-factor models and risk-balanced portfolios
* Hedge strategies using perpetual markets
* One-click strategy following and automated execution

#### **Integrated Benefits**

* Strategies use GLP depth for high-efficiency execution
* Risk management is embedded at the protocol level
* Ideal for passive users seeking automated returns

This module makes CloudBank accessible for users of all experience levels by turning proven quantitative models into simple, automated tools.
