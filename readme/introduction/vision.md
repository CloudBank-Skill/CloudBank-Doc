# Vision

CloudBank's vision is to build a community-defined, liquidity-driven global open financial system.

While traditional financial frameworks are centralized and siloed, and existing DeFi protocols concentrate market-making capabilities at the protocol layer, CloudBank returns financial sovereignty to users and communities.

The future global economy will not be dictated by centralized gatekeepers but will consist of tens of thousands of community-driven markets:

* Communities can deploy their own trading venues.
* Projects can define their own derivatives ecosystems.
* KOLs can own their own prediction zones.
* DAOs can establish their own governance financial systems.
