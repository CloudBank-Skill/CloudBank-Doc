# 🖥️ CloudBank

CloudBank is the world’s first community-driven, full-stack DeFi super application built on a **Dual Liquidity Architecture** (GLP + CLP). Its mission is to redefine how liquidity is created, owned, and governed in decentralized finance by allowing any community, project, or creator to launch their own markets, manage liquidity, and build autonomous financial ecosystems.

Unlike traditional DEXs that rely on a single liquidity model, CloudBank integrates **shared liquidity**, **community-owned liquidity**, and **multi-module financial applications** into one unified system. This architecture enables CloudBank to support both high-liquidity mainstream assets and long-tail community assets without compromising stability or scalability.

CloudBank brings together five major product modules:

* **Decentralized Exchange (DEX):** Spot and perpetual trading with aggregated depth
* **Launchpad:** Community-driven fundraising and token issuance
* **Prediction Markets:** Event-driven trading supported by robust pricing mechanisms
* **AI Quant Lab:** AI-powered trading strategies and portfolio automation
* **Payment Network:** Cross-asset settlement and crypto payment infrastructure

These modules operate on top of a foundational liquidity system that balances institutional-grade depth with grassroots market creation, enabling a new generation of open, customizable financial applications.

CloudBank aims to build the **next-generation decentralized financial infrastructure**, where liquidity is open, programmable, and defined by the community—not centralized actors.
